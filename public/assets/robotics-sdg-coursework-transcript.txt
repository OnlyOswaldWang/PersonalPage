Control weeds while protecting crops. Define the field boundary and plan the planting lines. The robot aligns with this plan. Each seed is placed and its position recorded. The robot uses this map on later visits. Positioning connects the machine to its seed map.

Commands depend on tool position and timing. The tool loosens soil between the crop lines. The loosened plants remain on the surface. Small knives enter gaps between neighbouring plants. They retract near stored crop positions. Mechanical weeding may reduce herbicide use.

Inspect the field and plan further control. Sunlight and workload affect battery endurance. Soil, weather and weed size affect performance. Compare remaining weeds and crop injury. Count all labour, energy and follow-up work. Position errors can direct tools towards crops.
